The rethought home — one living model, talk or touch

Not a linear chat wizard. The property model is the persistent home surface — always visible, directly editable. Conversation is one input; direct manipulation (tap → sheet) is the other. Progress is ambient (the rail up top), never gated. This is the IA the components above are built for.

Why this IA
  • Model always visible — the truth never scrolls away. Trust.
  • Talk or touch — beginners speak; the confident tap floors/rooms → sheets.
